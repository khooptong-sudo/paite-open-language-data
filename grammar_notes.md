# Paite Grammar Notes

Owner-curated notes from live conversation with Levia. These are working hypotheses, not a formal grammar.

## Pronouns

| Person | Subject | Object |
|--------|---------|--------|
| I | Ka | hon (?) |
| you | Na / Nang | Na hon / N'on |

- `Na hon` / `N'on` = "you" (object form).
- `hon` may be a general object marker / first-person object pronoun (= me).

## Verbs

| English | Paite |
|---------|-------|
| know | thei |
| love | it |
| see / look / check | en |
| do | chi / chim / chih ? |

- `didan` appears in "What are you doing?" — possibly an auxiliary or verb stem for "do".
- `chi` / `chim` seem related to "matter / thing / do".

## Particles / question markers

- `hia?` / `hiam?` = question marker (yes/no or open question).
- `e` = emphatic/assertive particle? (`Ka dam e.` = "I am well.")
- `le?` = question particle? (`Hiai te bang chih didan a le?`)
- `a` = verb/assertive particle? Common in biblical text.
- `te` = plural marker? (`Hiai te` = "these")

## Numbers and counting

The numeral follows the noun. The noun does not change for plural: `suang` (rock) stays the same with every number.

| Paite | English |
|-------|---------|
| Suang | rock / rocks |
| Suang khat | 1 rock |
| Suang nih | 2 rocks |
| Suang thum | 3 rocks |
| Suang nga | 5 rocks |
| Suang sawm | 10 rocks |
| Suang za / Suang zakhat | 100 rocks (`za` = hundred, `zakhat` = one hundred) |
| Suang sangkhat | 1,000 rocks (`sang` = thousand) |

Zero is not said with a number. It is said with a negative sentence:

| Paite | English |
|-------|---------|
| Leilak ah suang ka mukei. | I don't see any rocks on the ground. (`leilak ah` = on the ground) |
| Suang khat le ka mukei. | I don't see even one rock. |

- `kei` after the verb (`mu` = see) = negation? (`ka mukei` = I don't see)
- `le` after a quantity = "even"? (`suang khat le` = even one rock)

## Sample corrected sentences

| Paite | English |
|-------|---------|
| Hiai te bang chih didan a le? | What are we supposed to do with these? / What are you doing? |
| Na hon thei hia? | Do you know me? |

| N'on it hia? | Do you love me? |
| Ka email hon en mah. | Please check my email. |
| Ka email hon en dih. | Please check my email. (alternative) |
